Executive Summary

ClarifyAI is an AI-powered video companion that enables viewers to ask questions about any video content in real-time and receive contextually accurate, spoiler-free explanations.

We solve a universal problem: everyone gets confused watching content, but no one wants to admit it. Current solutions—Googling, asking friends, or rewinding—either spoil the experience or create social friction.

Our technology combines Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with temporal awareness to deliver instant explanations that never reveal future plot points.

Market Opportunity: $50B+ across streaming platforms (Netflix, HBO, Disney+), social video (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram), and educational content (Coursera, Udemy).

Business Model: B2B SaaS platform licensing + Creator subscription tools + Enterprise integrations.

The Ask: $1.5M seed round to build MVP, onboard 1,000 creators, and secure first platform partnership.

Current "Solutions" — And Why They Fail

1. Google It - User leaves the platform → engagement lost - Search results contain spoilers → experience ruined - Context is generic, not timestamped → still confused

2. Ask Someone - Social pressure → embarrassment - The other person might not know either - Interrupts their viewing experience too

3. Rewind and Rewatch - Time-consuming and frustrating - Might still not understand - Breaks immersion in the narrative

4. Give Up - User abandons content → lost customer - Negative word-of-mouth → reduced acquisition - Wasted content investment by studios

The Deeper Business Problem

Complex, prestige content is expensive to produce and drives subscriber retention. Yet it's also the content most likely to confuse viewers and cause churn.

Examples: - Tenet (Christopher Nolan): $200M budget, widely praised, but notoriously confusing - Dark (Netflix): 3-season epic, critical acclaim, high drop-off due to complexity - Westworld (HBO): Multi-timeline narrative loses casual viewers

Studios are trapped: Simplify content and lose critical acclaim, or maintain complexity and lose casual viewers.

ClarifyAI breaks this tradeoff.

Key Innovation: Temporal Spoiler Prevention

def retrieve_context(question: str, current_timestamp: float):
    """Only retrieve content from BEFORE the user's current position."""
    results = vector_db.query(
        query_text=question,
        filter={"timestamp": {"$lte": current_timestamp}},
        top_k=5
    )
    return results

This ensures the system is architecturally incapable of spoiling.
